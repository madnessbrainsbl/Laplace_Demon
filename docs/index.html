An honest predictor for finite universes — exact where knowledge is complete, candid where it is not.

A laboratory of finite models and numerical diagnostics. It does not prove that Laplace's demon is impossible under every law of nature. The Windows release includes the Python runtime and Go kernel.

The world computes. Watch it.

One ring of cells, one local law, deterministic evolution. This canvas draws every row. The desktop simulator can skip detected cycles; its GF(2) shortcut applies only to linear rules.

Outrun the demon

A hidden 31-cell world runs rule 30. You see one cell. Call its next bit. The demon never guesses — it computes. Every bit you fail to call was determined before you clicked.

The full laboratory

Eight tabs with explicit model assumptions. Finite examples illustrate limits; they do not prove universal physical impossibility.
Classical

Exact finite-model events, predecessor enumeration, GF(2) fast-forward for linear rules, and bounded cycle search for other rules.

From Events

The law is not given: the demon infers every rule consistent with an observed history and answers only as firmly as the evidence allows.

Continuous

RK4 mechanics, impact detection, finite-time growth estimates and step comparisons. The displayed horizon is a heuristic, not a guaranteed error bound.

Quantum (Go)

Density matrices, decoherence, Born probabilities, CHSH and inverse circuits. Measurement reports ignore the outcome; Bell violations constrain local hidden-variable models under Bell-test assumptions.

Formula L

L = I(E;K)/H(E) for a selected ensemble, minimal observed-cell sets, initial-state spatial masks and coarse-grained uncertainty. This does not derive the second law.

Active Inference

An agent buys knowledge cell by cell and becomes a demon — or stalls on XOR-like laws, where greedy curiosity provably overpays.

The Walls

Bekenstein's capacity upper bound, Landauer's reset-heat bound for unbiased bits, and finite illustrations of diagonal opposition and limits on mutual inference.

The Game

The demo above, plus animations of the hidden world stepping its law — randomness as a knowledge gap, experienced first-hand.
